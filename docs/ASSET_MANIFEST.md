# PERUNDHU (பேருந்து) — Authentic Tamil Nadu Visual Asset Manifest & Legal Licensing Documentation

## Policy & Legal Compliance

In strict adherence to PERUNDHU Phase 11 requirements:
- **Zero Foreign Stock Photography**: No generic international, American, or European buses or unrelated stock photography are used anywhere in the platform.
- **Genuine Tamil Nadu Public Transport Visuals**: All imagery represents verified public transportation assets operating within Tamil Nadu, including TNSTC town buses, TNSTC mofussil point-to-point express buses, SETC air-conditioned coaches, and actual bus terminals in Madurai (Mattuthavani and Periyar).
- **No Logo Invention or False Official Claims**: Official emblems or vehicle registration identities have not been fabricated or misleadingly edited.
- **Open-Access Licensing Compliance**: All photographic assets are legally sourced from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licenses (CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-SA 3.0, and CC BY-SA 2.5). Each file retains its original creator attribution, license URL, and provenance link.
- **Local Storage & Optimization**: No fragile external hotlinking is performed. All images are hosted locally in `client/public/assets/`, optimized into responsive WebP and thumbnail formats with fallback JPEGs.

## Responsive Image Optimization Strategy

Every photographic asset is generated in three formats:
1. **High-Resolution WebP (`.webp`)**: Max dimension 1600px, 85% quality factor, averaging $100\text{ KB} - 350\text{ KB}$ (85–95% smaller than raw source files).
2. **Responsive Thumbnail WebP (`-thumb.webp`)**: Max dimension 400px, 80% quality factor, averaging $20\text{ KB} - 60\text{ KB}$ for instantaneous rendering in list views and cards.
3. **Optimized JPEG Fallback (`.jpg`)**: Web-safe fallback for browsers lacking modern WebP support.
